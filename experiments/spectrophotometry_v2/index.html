AT THE BENCH

Direct laboratory controls

Pick up and pipette

Choose the pipette by its bulb and labeled stand. Use WATER (teal) for distilled water, rinses and the water blank. Use STOCK (terracotta) for Blue #1 stock and prepared dye standards. Use UNKNOWN (violet) for the original drink and its dilution. Drag the bulb or glass shaft. Keep holding as you drag the tip down through the source opening. You can also click once to carry the tool and press the opening directly. Return each pipette to its own stand before switching. Release once the tip is immersed and watch the meniscus rise. At the receiving opening, hold and drag down until the tip is seated. The meniscus falls as the receiving vessel fills. The tip stays seated until the dose is complete and you release. If you set the pipette down partway into a receiving tube, grasp the bulb and continue lowering it. Click the stand to put the pipette down.

Cuvette and instrument

Open the lid and click the cuvette to pick it up by its frosted sides. Hold over the chamber and move down to seat it. Release, close the lid and click ZERO or READ. To remove it, reopen the lid, hold the cuvette and drag up. Click or right-click the waste opening to tip the cuvette, then click it again to turn upright. Click its pad to put it down.

Views and targets

The thin rings mark the next target and the opening under the pointer. A gold ring and the instruction above the dock confirm contact. On empty bench space, left-drag sideways to orbit and up or down to tilt. Right-drag to pan up, down or sideways. Middle-drag or Shift + right-drag also orbits. Scroll to zoom toward the pointer. Choose Follow step to frame the tools for each operation, Bench for the complete workspace, or Show target for a close look at the next action. Arrange moves equipment between operations.

On a touchscreen, tap to pick up the tool, then press the opening and slide down. For keyboard or single-tap operation, open Targets, choose an item and use the action shown. The menu offers the next physical action for the selected item.

Hand gestures

Choose Hands, then Start hands. Click where your hand rests in the preview and select Use this frame. Select Tools. Open your palm over the pipette grip, then curl at least three fingers around it. The colored outline follows your hand.

Guided stroke. Guide the tip to the Blue #1 stock bottle. At the gold ring, dip your closed hand slightly and hold it there as the bulb draws liquid. For the second standard, lift when the pipette reads 8.0 mL. Carry it to the 8/2 tube, then dip and hold while it empties. Lift early to pause a dose. Dip again to finish it. Open your palm to put the pipette down.

Manual squeeze. In Hands, set Pipette bulb to Manual squeeze. Keep your middle, ring and little fingers curled. Above the source, pinch thumb and index, lower the tip while pinching, then spread thumb and index to draw. Lower the filled pipette into the receiving vessel, then pinch and hold to dispense.

Turn your wrist to tip the cuvette. Close and reopen your hand over a lid, instrument key or clipboard to use it. Camera images stay on this device.

Recovery and keyboard

Tracking loss, Pause and Stop freeze the object and stop liquid movement, preserving partial volumes. Choose Run and pick up the tool again to resume. Stop and Reset also turn the camera off. Reset clears the experiment while retaining the bench layout. On the focused bench, Enter picks up the next tool or aligns a held tool. Arrow keys move, lower and lift. Space operates the bulb. Escape releases the tool. The same physical prerequisites apply.

Model and limits

This instructional quantitative simulation preserves the source 2.31 µM stock, 10 mL dilution series, 3 mL cuvette aliquots and 630 nm teaching readings. Transfers conserve volume and source components at each increment. All input methods move liquid at up to 3 mL/s. Smaller doses flow more slowly so you can follow the meniscus. The label beside it shows the volume in the pipette. The model simplifies bulb pressure and air compression. The opening guides help center the tip during insertion. Equipment collisions and spills are not simulated.

Fit A = mC through the origin after blanking. The original drink absorbs more than the strongest standard. Plan a 10 mL dilution, rinse the cuvette, prepare the mixture in the graduated cylinder and measure the diluted drink. Ideal mixing and additive volumes are assumed. At 630 nm the drink’s absorbance scales with its volume fraction. Use C = A/m, then multiply by the dilution factor.
