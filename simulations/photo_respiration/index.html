Cellular Energy Lab

Experience the beautiful dance of photosynthesis and cellular respiration. Watch molecules transform as energy flows through the cell.

1
Spawn Water (H₂O) and Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) molecules from the left panel
2
Drag molecules into the Chloroplast during daytime to perform photosynthesis
3
Products auto-route: Glucose and O₂ fly to your inventory, ready for the next step
4
Feed them to the Mitochondrion to generate ATP energy!
5
Toggle Day/Night mode to control when photosynthesis can occur
6
Watch the Homeostasis Dashboard. Your cell's Na⁺/K⁺ pumps, protein folding, and DNA repair all consume ATP!
