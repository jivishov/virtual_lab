The hand-building feature uses Google's MediaPipe Tasks Vision JavaScript and WebAssembly distribution (@mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.0.1) and MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (float16, version 1). The original runtime and model are bundled for local inference.
